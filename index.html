The Problem

Home nursing still lives in the past

The daily work of nursing staff during home visits is still full of technical friction.

Paper and pen

Notes are taken by hand and transcribed later to a computer.

Unclear results

Existing software does not clearly show all patterns or scales.

No trend visibility

There is no visual tool to follow the patient over time.

The Solution

A mobile app that digitizes the entire visit

UbuNurse brings Gordon's Functional Health Patterns and assessment scales to the nurse's pocket, works offline, and syncs data automatically when WiFi is available.

Patient management

Detailed patient view with full history and quick search.

11 integrated patterns

All Gordon functional patterns and their associated scales.

Trend charts

Clear visualization of patient evolution with Chart.js.

User flow

From the patient's door to insight in minutes

UbuNurse removes manual steps from the home-visit workflow.

1

Open the patient

Search the patient or open the daily list.

2

Choose pattern and scale

Pick the proper Gordon pattern and scale.

3

Complete the questionnaire

The app guides you with structured questions and semantic colors.

4

Review evolution and save

Check the chart and sync when connectivity returns.
